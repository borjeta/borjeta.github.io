DomoGarden

Know when and how much to water each plot of your vegetable garden.

  • Daily watering plan per plot, in gallons or liters, from your crop, your soil and real weather data (FAO-56 method).
  • Only confirmed rain counts: log it with your own rain gauge, or let satellite data fill it in.
  • 95 crops, from tomatoes and lettuce to fruit trees, with ground beds, raised beds and pots.
  • Planting calendar for USDA hardiness zones 5–9 and for Spain.
  • No account and no hardware. Your garden data stays on your phone.
